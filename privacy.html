Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

QuoteCraft ("we", "our") is an online tool to create quotations and invoices. This page explains what information we use.

Information we collect

When you log in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We use them only to create your account and to know whether you have a Premium plan.

Your quotations and business details

The quotations, client details, logos and signatures you create are stored on your own device (in your browser). We do not sell or share them.

Payments

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

How we use information

To run your account, give Premium access, apply coupons and answer your support requests. We do not sell your data and do not show third-party ads based on it.

Services we use

Supabase (login and account data), Google (sign-in) and Razorpay (payments).

Deleting your data

Email us at quotecraft79@gmail.com from your registered email and we will delete your account data.
